## 👋 About Me

I'm **Ghislain Kongolo**, a full-stack engineer with 5+ years of experience building scalable web platforms, mobile apps and APIs that solve real-world problems, from healthcare and insurance to logistics and marketplaces.

- 💊 Developed **PharmaCD** — a SaaS pharmacy management platform for inventory and sales, with free and Pro plans and mobile money / bank payments
- 🛒 Building [**G-Shop**](https://github.com/Ghis8/G-Shop) — a mobile shopping app
- 🏢 Currently working on a multi-tenant insurance management platform (NestJS, MongoDB, React) and a ride-sharing and vehicle-rental marketplace (React Native, Expo, Node.js, Prisma)
- 📱 Shipping **iOS and Android** apps with React Native and Expo, and growing my native **Android** skills with Kotlin
- 🔐 Strong focus on role-based access control, multi-tenant data separation and clean API design
- 🌍 Based in **Kigali, Rwanda** — working remotely with international teams in English
- ⚡ Night shift is always on. No exceptions.
